# Next Steps   
  
Your WhatsApp account is ready. You can now start using its features and exploring its settings.   

- **Start a chat**: Send a text message to a contact.
- **Make a call**: Get in touch with a contact through a video or voice call.
- **Share a status update**: Share a photo or video as your Status.
- **Follow channels**: Explore channels and follow those that interest you.
- **Explore various settings**: Explore settings such as appearance, privacy, and notifications to enhance your WhatsApp experience.
