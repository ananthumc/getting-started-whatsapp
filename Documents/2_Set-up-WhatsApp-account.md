# Set up your WhatsApp account   

The following instructions describe how you can set up your WhatsApp account.   

**Prerequisite**   

-   An active phone number that can receive SMS messages or phone calls.   

To set up your WhatsApp account,   

1.	Open WhavtsApp.
2.	On the welcome screen, select your preferred language and tap the right arrow icon.
3.	Tap **Agree and continue**.
4.	From the drop-down menu, select your country. 
5.	Enter your phone number.
**Note:** WhatsApp automatically fills the calling code of your country.
6.	Tap **Next**.
A phone number verification screen appears.
A six-digit verification code is sent to your device as a text message.
7.	Enter the verification code.
8.	Perform one of the following steps:   
    - Tap **Continue** to allow WhatsApp to access your contacts and media.
    - Tap **Skip** to deny access.   
  If you used WhatsApp previously, a screen for restoring your backup appears.
9.	Perform one of the following steps:   
    - Select **Continue** to allow WhatsApp access your Google Drive and search for a backup.
If WhatsApp finds a backup, select **Restore**. 
Wait for restoration to complete. 
    - Select **Cancel** to skip the backup-restoring process.
10.	 On the next screen, tap the text field to enter your name.
11.	 (Optional) Tap the plus icon and add a profile picture.
12.	 Tap **Next**.
A screen appears asking you to allow WhatsApp access your contacts.
13.	 Tap **Settings** and then tap **Allow** to give WhatsApp access to your contacts.

The **Chats** screen appears. Your WhatsApp account is now ready.
